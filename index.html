See what your money choices would leave you with.

Can I? helps you compare ways to use your money before you decide. Enter the money you have now, protected savings, upcoming expenses, and debts.

  • Help me decide (What should I pay?) Compare ways to divide the same amount between debts, bills, purchases, and savings.
  • Test my plan (What if?) Try one or more actions together and see how your balances would change.

Start by entering your numbers below, or choose Try sample numbers to explore. Comparing options does not move money or change your saved balances.

Comparisons use the information you enter. Expected income is not cash until received.
Explanations use rules in this version. No live AI or bank connection.
